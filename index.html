The $Maru Narrative

Maru Coin is inspired by the happiest and most famous dog in the world — Japan's legendary Shiba Inu, Maru. This cheerful and loyal dog has been the center of crazy viral content since 2008.

Here's a timeline of the Maru meme:

2007 – Maru is born in Japan

As a pure-bred Shibu, Maru's loving owner raises him to become a cute, funny and charismatic dog.

2008 – Maru's owner, Shinjiro Ono, begins posting photos and videos of Maru online

Ono's Ameba Blog and YouTube channel helped him attract early fans. Maru goes absolutely viral in Japan almost immediately.

2010–2014 – Maru becomes internationally famous

Maru's YouTube channel reaches millions of views. People start naming him “the most cheerful dog in the world.” Maru is featured in Japanese magazines, advertisements, and even calendars. He becomes one of the most-followed dogs on Instagram and YouTube during this era.

2015–2020 – Maru’s brand becomes a cultural icon

Books and photo albums featuring Maru are published in Japan and abroad. His face is used in products, stationery, and home goods. He maintains a loyal following despite the rise of newer dog influencers.

Maru is now universally recognized as the "Second Most Famous Dog in the World." Maru Coin captures all of this awesome energy in a digital asset with a cult following.
