Marutaro was a famous Japanese Shiba Inu known for his large following on social media, particularly Instagram, where he shared his daily adventures with millions of fans. 

Adopted by Shinjiro Ono on Christmas Eve, "Marutaro" (meaning "circle" in Japanese) rose to internet fame after Ono began posting photos of their travels. Marutaro passed away in January 2024 at the age of 16, but his memory and the happiness he brought to people around the world live on, with people sharing their fond memories and favorite moments.
